DIGESTICALME DOMAIN SETUP

Cloudflare Pages custom domain:
  get.digesticalme.com

Shopify domain:
  digesticalme.com

The root Shopify domain owns /cart, /checkout and /policies.
The get subdomain serves only the paid-traffic landing page.

In Cloudflare Pages add get.digesticalme.com through the project's Custom domains screen.
Do not point digesticalme.com at the Pages project.
